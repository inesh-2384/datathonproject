## 1. Mathematical Formulation

Control Margin $M(t) \in [0, 100]$ represents the remaining buffer of operational controllability available to steer a stressed financial network back to equilibrium before hitting an unrecoverable horizon.

### Exact Additive Decomposition:
$$M(t) = \text{clamp}\left( M_0 + \Delta_{\text{dep}}(t) + \Delta_{\text{prop}}(t) + \Delta_{\text{herd}}(t) + \Delta_{\text{infra}}(t) + \Delta_{\text{rec}}(t) + \Delta_{\text{res}}(t), \, 0, \, 100 \right)$$

Where:
- $M_0 = 91.0$: Authoritative baseline buffer in normal equilibrium.
- $\Delta_{\text{dep}} \le 0$: Penalty for structural concentration on distressed or compromised data/information providers and chokepoints.
- $\Delta_{\text{prop}} \le 0$: Penalty for multi-hop cascade propagation depth and breadth of affected nodes.
- $\Delta_{\text{herd}} \le 0$: Penalty for collective algorithmic correlation and temporal decision synchrony.
- $\Delta_{\text{infra}} \le 0$: Penalty for core settlement/clearing infrastructure node failure (RTGS, CCP).
- $\Delta_{\text{rec}} \ge 0$: Margin bonus generated by active, verified containment and recovery protocols.
- $\Delta_{\text{res}} \le 0$: Penalty for unaddressed latent contagion or tainted secondary assets.

## 4. Double-Counting Audit (Section 12)

Hostile reviewers question whether **Dependency Concentration** and **Propagation Depth** independently penalize the same underlying risk.

### Causal Accounting Map:
1. **$\Delta_{\text{dep}}$ accounts for structural topology:** It penalizes the *in-degree centrality and market share* of the compromised provider (e.g. DATA-X serving 4 G-SIBs). This measures static structural exposure.
2. **$\Delta_{\text{prop}}$ accounts for dynamic cascade transmission:** It penalizes *actual multi-hop transmission across interbank lending rails* (Bank A $\to$ Bank B $\to$ Bank C).
3. **Orthogonality Check:**
   - If DATA-X fails but interbank bilateral limits are severed, $\Delta_{\text{dep}} = -22$, but $\Delta_{\text{prop}} = 0$.
   - If DATA-X is stable but Bank A suffers operational outage and defaults on Bank B, $\Delta_{\text{dep}} = 0$, but $\Delta_{\text{prop}} = -12$.
4. **Conclusion:** The two metrics are mathematically orthogonal: one measures structural source concentration, while the other measures multi-hop graph percolation. Zero double-counting exists.
